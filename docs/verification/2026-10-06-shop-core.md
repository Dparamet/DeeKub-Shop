## Public UI observations

Observed through the in-app browser, using a separate preview tab:

- Catalog displays the six seeded products from the API.
- Search for Hades returns one product; a nonexistent title displays the empty state and reset action.
- Steam + Global filtering returns three games. Ascending prices are Stardew Valley 315, Hades II 690 and Cyberpunk 2077 1,290 baht. A 500-baht maximum leaves Stardew Valley only.
- Saving Hades places it in the wishlist.
- Product detail shows platform, region, available stock, price and a quantity control.
- Two Hades units in the cart display a total of 1,380 baht. Anonymous checkout redirects to `/login?next=/cart`.
- Anonymous `/admin` redirects to `/login?next=/admin`.
- Public storefront console observations returned no warning/error entries.

These observations do not establish authenticated checkout or admin behavior.

## UI quality gate

Direction and purpose are recorded in `docs/DESIGN.md`: existing deeKub dark/cyan identity, abstract illustrations labeled as placeholders, browsing controls inspired by Loaded, and ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

Source review includes loading/empty/error states, 44px controls, visible focus, responsive CSS, explicit accessible names for icon actions, and stronger form borders. No invented customer metrics or fulfillment claims were added.

**Gate status: incomplete.** Authenticated controls, email recovery, USER denial, ADMIN editing, and full keyboard/mobile click-through remain unobserved. The antislop R-35 and resilience checks cannot be marked passed until these flows are exercised with a confirmed account. No claim of production readiness or full acceptance is made.
